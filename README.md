## Building Daybreak

A calm, local-first study planner for choosing the next task, protecting focus time, and reviewing progress.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### One next step
Today shows one realistic next action, with everything else quietly below it.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### Protected focus
25, 50 or 90-minute sessions. Soft rain or brown noise plays only after you press start.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### Room to think
Optional, consent-based AI study support. It explains and quizzes. It never writes your graded work.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

Runs in the browser on IndexedDB. No account, private by default, works offline. English and Thai.

## Principles

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Offline first** | Core workflows stay useful offline, and data recovery is straightforward. |
| **Visible consent** | Consent and data boundaries are never hidden. |
| **Small and real** | Small changes, clear interfaces, checks that exercise real behavior. |
| **Accessible by default** | Accessibility, readable code and predictable failure are part of the design. |
